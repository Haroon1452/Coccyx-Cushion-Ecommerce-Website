# Coccyx Cushion – Project Overview

## 1. Introduction

Coccyx Cushion is a UK-focused e-commerce brand specializing in ergonomic memory foam seat cushions designed to improve everyday sitting comfort.

The store offers seating support products for customers who spend extended periods sitting at work, at home, while driving, or travelling.

**Website:** https://coccyx-cushion.co.uk/

## 2. Project Objectives

The objective of this project is to provide an online shopping platform that presents ergonomic comfort products through an accessible, customer-focused storefront.

Key objectives include:

- Establishing an online retail presence.
- Presenting ergonomic cushion products.
- Organizing products and bundle offers.
- Communicating product features and benefits.
- Providing an intuitive shopping experience.
- Supporting customer inquiries and order information.
- Serving customers primarily in the UK market.

## 3. Business Overview

Coccyx Cushion operates in the e-commerce and personal comfort products category.

Its primary product is a memory foam coccyx cushion featuring an ergonomic U-shaped design.

### Product Categories and Use Cases

- Office and work-from-home comfort.
- Pregnancy and postpartum comfort.
- Driving and travel support.
- Everyday seating comfort.
